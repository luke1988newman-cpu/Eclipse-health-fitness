Gym questions, answered

Where is Eclipse Health & Fitness?

Eclipse is at 140 Station Rd, Hesketh Bank, Preston PR4 6SR. We serve Hesketh Bank, Tarleton, Banks, Croston, Bretherton, Longton, Much Hoole, Burscough and the wider north west of Preston and Ormskirk area.

What are the gym opening hours?

Monday to Friday 6am to 9pm. Saturday and Sunday 8am to 3pm.

How much is gym membership in Hesketh Bank?

Off-peak membership is £29 a month, for using the gym 7am to 3pm. Total Eclipse, gym and classes at any time we are open, is £33 a month and does not include speciality classes. Joint membership with Ministry of Fitness is £65 a month.

Do you run fitness classes?

Yes. Total Eclipse includes classes, and our sister gym Ministry of Fitness runs coached group fitness classes for all levels. You can try Ministry of Fitness for seven days for £7.

Can I train with a personal trainer?

Yes. Harry, Helen, Alfie, Aaron and Nicola are personal trainers at Eclipse. Call 01772 379293 or send an enquiry.

How do I join?

Join online in about two minutes, or call in and ask at the desk.
